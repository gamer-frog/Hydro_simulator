GROWER HANDBOOK

How to Play

🌱 1 · Plant

Click a grow bed to select it, pick a crop, and watch it germinate. Leaves like Lettuce thrive in NFT channels, fruiting crops (Tomato, Pepper, Cucumber) need Dutch buckets, Strawberries love Vertical Towers.

💧 2 · Care

Each bed sips nutrient solution. Keep pH between 5.5–6.5 and EC near the crop's ideal band. If the tank runs dry or chemistry drifts off, growth stalls and plants sicken. Use the dose buttons — or buy the Auto-Doser and relax.

📈 3 · Sell

Harvested produce goes to your warehouse. Prices move daily — open Market (M) to sell, or lock in premiums via Contracts (C). Reputation makes buyers pay more.

🏭 4 · Expand

Build (B) lets you place more beds anywhere in the greenhouse. Reinvest in LEDs, HVAC, rainwater harvesting and marketing. Weather shifts daily — heatwaves punish unshaded crops.
